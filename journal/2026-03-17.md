- Scores: gvm-mock-server 7.7, gvm-client 7.6, gvm-connection 7.6, gvm-gmp 7.5, gvm-protocol 7.5
- Key gaps: no supplier names (0/10 NTIA), CycloneDX 1.3 (5/10 BSI), no build lifecycle, no SBOM data license, no multi vuln lookup IDs

### syft Comparison
- Installed `syft v1.42.2` and benchmarked against `cargo-cyclonedx`
- syft scored **5.6/10** (worse overall) but excelled at: CycloneDX 1.6 (10/10), CPE identifiers (9.8/10), executable URIs (9.1/10)
- syft lacked: license resolution (0/10), SHA-256 checksums (0.1/10), source URIs (0/10)
- Recommendation: hybrid approach — keep cargo-cyclonedx as primary, post-process to add syft's strengths
- Findings posted to [issue #20 comment](https://github.com/clawosiris/rust-gvm/issues/20#issuecomment-4075828470)
